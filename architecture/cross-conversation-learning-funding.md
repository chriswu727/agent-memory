# Private historical learning capacity

Fresh ordinary encoding roots carry a server-issued `private-brain-history-funding-v1` permission. After encoding, their complete unused history allocation can fund an older, never-attempted historical comparison in another conversation of the same private brain, owner and branch. This allows work to progress when a conversation is abandoned and the owner continues elsewhere.

The transfer changes resource accounting only. The recipient retains its original conversation, input revisions, source hashes, historical cutoff, admitted objectives and encoding parent. The donor's text is not added to that comparison's inputs or evidence. Ordinary authorized cross-conversation retrieval is unchanged; a new conversation can still legitimately recall an older private memory.

Each ordinary root still authorizes at most four cognitive calls and 393,216 input bytes, with at most two calls and 196,608 input bytes for history. A donor must have a complete unused history pair and sufficient byte capacity. Separate one-call leftovers are never combined. Actual operations, including unknown outcomes, remain charged to their original authorization root. This is scheduling existing finite capacity, not granting another allowance.

The scheduler validates the complete recipient source package under the current policy before freezing it. The source-free funding origin binds the original deferred job, its immutable obligation manifest and the manifest admitted for this allocation. Reservation rechecks the origin, assigned job, owner policy, branch and operation ledger. Brain and job locks serialize assignment. An approved zero-cost replan can transfer the same unused assignment without changing its budget. Failed or uncertain paid comparisons are not automatically retried; independently unattempted remainder may continue.

Migration 022 makes permission, origin, historical obligation and logical scope immutable, including absent-to-present updates. Legacy roots retain exact-conversation authority. Recovery roots do not acquire cross-conversation permission, and explicit recovery strips donor permission/origin. Restored jobs remain subject to recovery quarantine; permission metadata does not authorize their automatic execution. Erased or corrected sources must pass the existing exact-version checks before any replacement can spend.

Public job monitoring reports `history_funding` only for validated cross-conversation lineage. It contains the fixed version, `authorization_root_job_id` and `origin_job_id`. Logical progress still follows the original encoding parent, while operation accounting follows the donor. Private manifests, endpoint identities and source text are not included in this summary. `history_attention` describes the original bounded admission, not a sum over descendant jobs; `search_exhaustive` remains false even when admitted work finishes.

Progress requires a later eligible root, a complete source package and available brain-wide capacity. No idle timer creates fresh paid allowance. A comparison whose required source has been erased, whose prior paid outcome is uncertain, or whose complete package exceeds current bounds remains blocked or deferred. Completion of admitted comparisons does not mean every possible historical comparison was discovered or evaluated.
