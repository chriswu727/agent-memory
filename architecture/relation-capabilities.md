# Versioned relation capabilities and speech-event attribution

New semantic snapshots use `relation-capabilities-v1`, inheriting source-backed episode navigation, fidelity assessment, operation scope and communication-intent rules. Previously frozen versions keep their serialized requests and guards. This contract does not reinterpret stored memories or retry paid failures.

Before generation, a deterministic table describes structural restrictions for each exact supplied memory version. It lists evidence-compatible claim kinds, unavailable relations with fixed reasons, conditional relations and eligible independent abstraction partners. The table shares the authoritative source and abstraction gates; it does not infer missing attribution, conditions or time metadata. Assistant speech cannot support an inferred-pattern abstraction. Equivalent claims require complete identical attribution, conditions and time metadata.

Capabilities are not truth judgments or permission to skip evidence. Every proposal must still pass complete source and endpoint-union checks, independent semantic assessment and publication validation. Additional proposal evidence can make an otherwise compatible endpoint union invalid. A negative or uncertain assessment cannot be overridden by the table. A legitimate no-change decision remains available. Projection and ranking use no provider calls, and the complete table is charged to existing request limits.

## Delivered conditional speech

The preserved history-000 failure attributed a conditional invitation to the assistant: “如果你需要我基于这个安排做计划或提醒，请告诉我。” The old lexical world check classified its content as hypothetical and rejected a real-world report that the assistant had said it.

Under the new contract, only an `agent_said` claim may distinguish that delivered speech event from its hypothetical content. Its source must be an actually delivered assistant message, with an `unknown` or `real_world_report` interpretation hint; the source content must otherwise classify as hypothetical. The candidate remains an attributed inference and retains the complete condition. It is not an external fact, an established condition, a promise or evidence that an action happened.

Explicit hypothetical, roleplay, dream and quotation source hints still constrain the world. Other claim kinds, partial or unconfirmed delivery, non-assistant sources, partial semantic units and incompatible parent worlds do not receive this exception. Claims, relation evidence, capability projection and publication use the same versioned guard. Independent verification remains required and fallible. The regression replays both original requests exactly, then tests the candidate under the new contract without a provider call; it does not claim a new real-model verdict.
