# Bounded encoding and historical integration

This batch addresses the sustained-learning failure preserved in the [stopped long-history experiment](deepseek-2026-10-25-long-history-stopped.md). It separates encoding new communication from comparing it with older memories. First-release acceptance remains open until a separately recorded real-provider evaluation verifies sustained behavior and independently reviews semantic quality.

## Implemented behavior

A fresh encoding job can withhold incomplete optional history before generation and encode only its complete new inputs. These results remain attributed reports and provisional interaction fragments. They cannot establish a current rule, resolve a historical contradiction, or prove an external action completed. Publication, processing progress, and the durable historical-comparison obligation commit together.

Historical integration selects complete, finite comparison packages. Source, parent, exception, and relation dependencies stay together. Proposal and verification use the same complete context; the bounded verification contract stores full records once and resolves exact references against them. Discovery excludes already attempted endpoints before candidate limits. Truncated discovery remains unresolved, including source bridges whose remaining seeds are outside the finite scan.

Each fresh encoding allocation allows up to two encoding calls and one additional historical proposal/verification pair: four calls and 393,216 input bytes in total. The existing per-brain rolling limit still applies. Failed and uncertain operations consume allocation. New independent inputs may contribute their unused historical pair to older, never-attempted comparisons; they cannot automatically fund another attempt at a failed comparison.

An invalidated task with no provider reservation can prepare a new immutable plan under the same allocation. Exact original inputs and comparison identities must remain available. Corrections and deletion cannot resurrect old content or replenish spent allocation. A structured failed historical task may delegate only its untouched remaining comparisons. Its attempted, unassessed comparisons remain visible even if the continuation finishes.

Encoding records an immutable attempted range so that invalidating a paid prefix does not strand its untouched suffix. Only the suffix can receive a continuation; the attempted prefix remains blocked. Explicit recovery separately freezes the current configured policy once while retaining the exact source plan. Correcting or deleting that plan prevents both repeated explicit recovery and automatic zero-call replacement from substituting different inputs.

New encoding jobs also use `episode-fidelity-v1`. Episode verification must separately support attribution, modality, specificity, and the interaction boundary. The contract rejects a missing or unsupported facet; it does not establish that a model's positive judgment is correct. Older frozen requests retain their prior contracts and serialized inputs.

## Remaining limits

Preflight checks the known minimum complete verification context; arbitrary generated proposals may still exceed the request budget. Such output cannot publish without verification. Finite candidate discovery is not exhaustive history search. Database filtering may inspect more rows than the returned candidate count.

Opaque failures outside structured finalization stay blocked. Explicit retry of a paid parent that already delegated its untouched tail is currently rejected to prevent duplicate ownership; selected-only operator recovery remains unsupported. A published relation can coexist with an unassessed overall comparison, and monitoring preserves that distinction.

This report does not replace the failed real-model evidence or claim improved long-term semantic quality. The next evaluation must record both encoding and historical-integration outcomes, retain unresolved debt, enforce persistent call ceilings, and preserve all earlier reports. No routine CI or subscription-backed tests are part of this batch.

Subsequent real-provider evidence on commit `4f1a41d`: the [fixed episode diagnostic](deepseek-2026-10-25-episode-fidelity.md) completed sixteen calls with known usage, but both verification contracts falsely accepted the same two recorded semantic errors. Its result does not invalidate the local engineering checks above; it blocks claiming improved episode fidelity from the four-facet contract.
